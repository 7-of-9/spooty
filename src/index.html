What identifies a recording?

ISRC identifies the intended recording. Spotify IDs link to the exact catalog entries. A SHA-256 fingerprint identifies the exact downloadable file. You’ll find these in each track’s details and in the CSV.

These MP3s were sourced separately from Spotify. Matching catalog metadata and duration do not guarantee the same recording or master. BPM and key are analysis estimates: check them in your DJ software.
